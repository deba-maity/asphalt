NIGHTSHIFT adaptation, 2026:
- Evaluated and reduced mesh density for real-time rendering.
- Rescaled to a 4.7 metre body; rebuilt eight inexpensive PBR material groups.
- Removed separate badge, decorative stripe and plate meshes.
- Separated wheel steering, rolling geometry and stationary brake calipers.
- Corrected the original showcase wheel orientation using mirrored rear wheels.
- Omitted the original studio, lights, cameras, source rig and scripts.
