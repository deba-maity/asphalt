The touring model used for Apex GT and Eclipse V8 is adapted from BMW 1 Series M In Cycles by Mike Pan.

The model and its NIGHTSHIFT adaptation are available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. The adaptation changes mesh density, materials, scale and wheel articulation, and omits separate badge meshes and the original studio scene.
