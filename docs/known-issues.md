# Current acceptance limits - 0.8.0-rc.1

The current work is documented in [Passage and lower-power revision](passage-and-power.md). Older pass records describe earlier builds and remain available for provenance.

- **Rendered and human acceptance remains outstanding.** The Remote Desktop restriction was respected: no graphical game window, desktop gameplay, listening session or physical controller test. Headless contact, layout, animation and endurance checks do not establish visual quality or combat feel.
- **Target laptop and heat are unmeasured.** Lower power offers 540p/30 FPS without MSAA or sun shadows. Menus, pause and focus loss reduce unnecessary work. These are implemented budgets, not a measured temperature or native-GPU FPS result. Existing preferences are preserved; select the preset on the play machine.
- **Short focused passage.** The default Play route now explains Rayan's return and offers three gentler teaching echoes before the Cinder Warden. The lessons use the existing armored opponent with tuning and color variations. They are not three newly modeled bosses. Their difficulty and reading pace need human feedback.
- **Art scope.** The trial uses licensed armor, retargeted motion and courtyard assets. It still lacks the bespoke animation, faces, cinematics and world production of God of War or Wukong. The archived six-chapter campaign keeps its earlier procedural characters and environments. Opening the archive does not show the trial's revised art.
- **Devices and accessibility.** Headless tests cover logical UI bounds and input rules, but cannot establish physical mouse capture, controller comfort, audio clarity, visual contrast or screen-reader support. Keyboard/mouse rebinding is supported; controller mapping is fixed.
- **Distribution.** This revision has a verified unsigned Windows candidate. No signing identity or new public binary/site release is created by this pass. Source updates use the existing `ashen-vow` branch of `deba-maity/asphalt`. Android remains an internal experiment; this revision does not include a new APK or mobile acceptance.

Use [local acceptance](local-acceptance.md) and [benchmark instructions](benchmark.md) on the play computer. Preserve the build identity and chosen settings with any report; do not substitute older captures or historical test records for the current build.
