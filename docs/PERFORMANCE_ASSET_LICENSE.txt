ASHEN VOW adaptations: shield removed; normalized scale; new anatomical skin
weights and a 65-bone rig based on Quaternius CC0 rest data; A-to-T bind-pose
conversion; retargeted motion; runtime armor palettes; shared, losslessly
compressed texture maps. The artist does not endorse this game.

Applies to rayan.glb, warden.glb and textures/knight-*.png, including their
modified versions. Color, tangent-space normal, metallic and roughness maps
come from the artist's packed source. Roughness and metallic are combined into
glTF channels. No unrelated packed photographs are included.
